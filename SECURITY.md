# Security Policy

## Supported Projects
This organization hosts small utilities and scripts. We do not offer security SLAs.

## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you believe you’ve found a security issue in one of our repositories:

- Please email a private report to our [contact address](https://www.schenker-tech.de/en/imprint/).
- Include a minimal proof-of-concept and affected versions/commit.
- Do not open public issues for vulnerabilities.

We will respond on a best-effort basis and may credit reporters by handle if you consent.
